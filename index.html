Dell assistant

A private, personal assistant that runs on its owner's own home server.

Dell assistant is used by one person only: its owner. It is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up. It helps the owner with their own calendar, email, files and tasks when the owner asks for it.

What it does

  • Answers the owner's questions about their own Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive and Google Tasks.
  • Searches the owner's own documents on the home server.
  • Runs on hardware the owner controls; there is no hosted service.
